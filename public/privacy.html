Privacy policy

This policy explains what the Abhi Utility Android app ("the app") and its website collect, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

In short: most tools work only on your phone. The app does not show ads, does not sell your data and does not use your data for advertising. Data reaches our server only if you create an account or turn on SOS recording.

1. Data that stays on your phone

These are processed only on your device and are never sent to us:

  • PDFs, scans, Word, Excel, CSV and Zip files you open, edit or create.
  • App Lock settings and passwords, the apps you lock and their time limits.
  • What the accessibility service sees (see section 4).
  • Files you download, saved WhatsApp statuses and the downloader's link history.
  • Your SOS contacts and SOS settings.
  • The list of installed apps (used to choose apps for App Lock and the reel counter, and by the APK extractor).

2. Data we collect when you create an account

An account is optional. Without one, nothing in this section is collected.

3. SOS

SOS is off until you set it up. When it is triggered:

  • The app calls your SOS contacts using your phone's dialer.
  • If you turned on "Text my location", it reads your current location once and sends it to your SOS contacts by SMS from your own SIM (normal SMS charges apply).
  • If you turned on recording (requires an account), it records 30 seconds of video and sound, uploads it to our server with your location, and texts your contacts a private link. Anyone with the link can view it. Recordings and links are deleted automatically after {{SOS_LINK_DAYS}} days, or when you delete your account.

Location, camera and microphone are used only at the moment SOS is triggered, never in the background otherwise.

4. Accessibility service

If you turn it on (after an in-app explanation that asks for your consent), the app uses Android's Accessibility Service API for these features only:

  • App Lock: sees which app is opened, to ask for your password before a locked app is shown and to enforce the time limits you set.
  • Reel counter: sees scrolling in Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, Snapchat and similar apps to count reels and time spent.
  • Download button: when you tap it in a web browser, reads the address bar once to get the page link.
  • SOS: notices when the lock screen is shown, so an emergency call can be started from it.

It does not read your messages, passwords or what you type, and it does not record your screen. Screen content is never uploaded or shared. Only the daily reel counts described in section 2 are synced, and only if you are signed in. You can turn the service off at any time in Android Settings → Accessibility.

5. Permissions

  • Camera: document scanner, profile photo and SOS recording.
  • Microphone: sound of the SOS recording.
  • Location: SOS location message and recording only.
  • Phone: placing SOS calls and noticing when a call ends.
  • SMS: sending SOS alerts to your SOS contacts. The app never reads your messages.
  • Notifications, flashlight, vibration, biometrics: status notifications, the shake flashlight, SOS alerts and fingerprint unlock (your fingerprint never leaves Android's secure hardware).
  • Internet: account, sync, backups, downloads and the in-app browser.

6. Who we share data with

We do not sell or rent personal data. We share it only with:

  • Our hosting provider, which stores our server and database.
  • Our email provider, to send verification and password-reset emails.
  • Google Play, which processes subscriptions under Google's own privacy policy.
  • Your SOS contacts, who receive what you chose to send them.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

The document scanner uses Google ML Kit, which runs on your phone. Web pages you open in the in-app browser or downloader are loaded directly from those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

7. Security

All traffic to our server uses HTTPS. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Diary entries and backups can be encrypted on your phone with a password only you know.

8. How long we keep data

  • Account data, synced data and backups: until you delete them or your account.
  • SOS recordings and links: {{SOS_LINK_DAYS}} days.
  • Hashed email for the free trial and referrals: kept after account deletion so a trial can't be claimed twice. It can't be turned back into your email.
  • Server logs (IP address, time, request): kept for a short time for security and troubleshooting.

9. Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all data on our server at any time:

  • In the app: Profile → Delete account.
  • On the web: delete your account.

Data stored only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription; cancel it in the Play Store.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it or to delete it by emailing {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. You can also change your name and photo, and delete your account, yourself in the app.

12. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
