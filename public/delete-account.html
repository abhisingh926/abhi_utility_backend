Delete your account

This permanently deletes your Abhi Utility account: your email, name, login sessions and subscription records.

  • Data stored only on your phone (diary, tasks, reel history, PDFs) is not on our server; uninstall the app to remove it.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play or App Store subscription. Cancel it in the Play Store or in your iPhone settings first.
  • You can also delete your account inside the app: Settings → Account → Delete account.
